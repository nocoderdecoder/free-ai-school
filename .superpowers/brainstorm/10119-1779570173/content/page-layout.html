How should the Deals & Events page be laid out?

Both content types live here — events (Google I/O, OpenAI DevDay) and deals (acquisitions, mergers). Pick how they're presented.

A

Unified feed with type badges

All articles in one chronological list. Each entry has a colored badge — EVENT or DEAL — so readers can scan at a glance. Simple, no tabs.

anshul.ai/deals-events
EVENT May 14
Google I/O 2026: Everything That Matters for Business
~800 words · full coverage
DEAL May 12
Microsoft Acquires Inflection AI for $650M
~450 words · deal analysis
DEAL May 9
Salesforce Buys an AI Startup Nobody Heard Of
~400 words · deal analysis
B

Two tabs — Events / Deals

Separate tabs let readers go to what they care about. Events tab shows conference/product coverage, Deals tab shows M&A analysis. Cleaner separation, slightly more navigation.

anshul.ai/deals-events
Events
Deals

C

Two stacked sections on one page

Page scrolls through "Latest Events" first (2–3 cards), then "Latest Deals" below. No tabs — everything visible on one scroll. Good for when content volume is low initially.
