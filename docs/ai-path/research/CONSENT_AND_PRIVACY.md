# Consent and privacy note

Consent version: `2026-07-17.v1`

This is an operational research script, not legal advice. The study owner must confirm that it matches the organization's approved privacy notice before recruitment.

## Read aloud before the session

> We are testing an early, text-only AI learning adviser. We want to understand whether the questions, assessment, and 30-day plan are clear and useful. We are evaluating the product, not you. This is not a test, employment assessment, certificate, or professional recommendation.
>
> Please use a fictional or public-safe work example. Do not enter customer data, confidential company information, credentials, health information, financial account details, or other sensitive personal data. If sensitive information appears, we will stop and remove it from the research notes.
>
> We will not use a live voice model, upload microphone audio, enroll you in a course, or make a purchase. Recording is off by default. The moderator will take notes using a participant code rather than your name. Exact typed responses, if retained for assessment review, will stay in the approved restricted research location and will not be placed in analytics, source control, issue trackers, or ordinary logs.
>
> Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question, stop at any time, or ask us to delete the session notes before synthesis without giving a reason. Choosing not to participate will not affect any service or relationship. Do you consent to continue with note-taking under these conditions?

Record only `consented` or `declined` against the opaque participant code. A decline ends the session and creates no product data.

## Optional follow-up consent

> May we contact you once in about seven days to ask whether you started or completed the first plan action? This is optional and separate from today's session.

Record only `follow_up_yes` or `follow_up_no`. Contact details remain in the approved recruitment system, not in the research packet.

## Data handling

| Data | Allowed location | Default handling |
|---|---|---|
| Contact and scheduling | approved recruitment system | never export to repository packet |
| Opaque participant code | research packet | retain with de-identified synthesis |
| Moderator coded observations | restricted research folder | retain through decision review |
| Exact typed answers/report | restricted research folder only | delete within 30 days after synthesis unless a shorter approved policy applies |
| Audio/video/screen recording | none by default | requires separate explicit consent and approved storage |
| Aggregate counts/themes | synthesis artifact | retain without PII or invented quotes |

The 30-day research-material schedule is a proposed maximum for this study; use a shorter organizational requirement when applicable. Product session retention remains governed separately by the versioned product notice.

## Incident procedure

If sensitive or identifying data is entered:

1. Stop the task and tell the participant why.
2. Do not copy the content into notes, chat, issues, or logs.
3. Delete the affected local preview/session using the available product control.
4. Mark `sensitive_data_stop` and whether deletion was confirmed; do not describe the data.
5. Notify the study owner through the approved incident channel.
