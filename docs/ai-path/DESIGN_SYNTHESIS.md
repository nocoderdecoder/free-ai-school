# AI Path Diagnostic Studio — design synthesis

## Product constraint

AI Path is a one-screen dual-path diagnostic. A learner either brings a specific
AI use case or asks for an evidence-based capability-growth prescription. Voice
and typing populate the same fields. The only next scene is the generated result.

## Twenty specialist ideas considered

### Product and visual design

1. **Living Field Guide** — editorial question sections with an evidence index.
2. **Decision Canvas** — answers visibly assemble into a diagnostic system map.
3. **Kinetic Interview Sheet** — one continuous sheet with an emphasized active section.
4. **Instrument Panel** — behaviorally anchored, tactile professional controls.

### Motion and interaction

5. **Diagnostic Workbench** — path changes reorganize the same workspace.
6. **Living Index** — a moving bracket and human-readable completion summaries.
7. **Voice Margin** — field-level capture without a separate voice journey.
8. **Blueprint Assembly** — form summaries reorganize into the result scene.

### Diagnostic form design

9. **Forked Diagnostic Canvas** — two retained path drafts and six visible sections.
10. **Embedded Decision Controls** — structured answers before long prose.
11. **Voice Form Copilot** — speech maps into the same versioned form fields.
12. **Evidence-Aware Readiness** — missing evidence, not fake percentage progress.

### Code-native graphics

13. **Working Ledger** — a thin diagnostic spine becomes the result timeline.
14. **Diagnostic Loom** — six evidence strands gather into skill, project and first move.
15. **Workpath Instrument** — one angular route connects the diagnostic stations.
16. **Evidence Aperture** — rectangular framing guides converge on the recommendation.

### Accessibility, responsive design and performance

17. **Semantic Conversation Spine** — predictable focus and one normalized state.
18. **Mode Console** — voice changes input behavior, never the questionnaire.
19. **Scene Continuity** — one restrained transform/opacity transition at a time.
20. **Lightweight Result Scene** — project and first action first, details lazy and secondary.

## Chosen direction

The implementation combines the **Working Ledger**, **Living Index**,
**Diagnostic Workbench**, **Embedded Decision Controls**, **Voice Margin**, and
**Blueprint Assembly** into one system called the **AI Path Diagnostic Studio**.

- Warm limestone surface, dark ink and acid-lime operational accent.
- Editorial headings paired with compact monospaced signal labels.
- A sticky six-signal index on desktop and horizontal workpath on mobile.
- Six full, visible and editable sections; no wizard and no chat transcript.
- Chamfered choices with behavioral anchors and conditional evidence fields.
- A global local microphone check plus field-level voice affordances.
- Path changes use a short content reorganization, preserving both drafts.
- The result scene keeps the same visual workpath and rearranges it into a
  four-week plan, making the recommendation feel derived rather than generated.

## Motion and performance budgets

- No third-party animation runtime.
- Transform/opacity transitions only, normally 180–240 ms and never over 420 ms.
- At most two scene elements animate simultaneously.
- Reduced-motion mode removes translation and uses an optional short fade.
- Audio visualization is local-only and capped by the existing controller.
- No canvas, animated blur, decorative media, glowing orb, chat bubble, or mesh gradient.

## Acceptance boundary

- Exactly two learner intents and no more than six top-level sections per intent.
- Every input is available on one scrollable scene and remains editable before submission.
- Voice and typing share field identifiers and form state.
- Higher capability claims require described evidence.
- Missing evidence is unassessed, never converted into a low skill score.
- Use-case and capability paths produce structurally different results.
- Every result contains a measurable definition of done and at most three resources.
- No paid service is activated by the diagnostic.
